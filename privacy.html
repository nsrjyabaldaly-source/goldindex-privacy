What the app does

GoldIndex (مؤشر الذهب) tracks gold, silver, platinum, and palladium prices, currencies, and selected market indicators. It does not offer trading or a bank account.

Developer

This app is developed by Mohanad Jiab.

Data stays on your device

We do not create a user account, and we do not collect your name, email, phone number, or location. We do not sell your data, and we do not share your personal information with third parties.

Stored only on the phone:

  • The language, currency, and display unit you choose.
  • Price alerts you create.
  • The last prices that were loaded, so they remain visible if the connection drops.

Uninstalling the app removes this data from the device.

Internet

The app uses the internet permission to load public market prices from price-data providers. A price request does not include your personal identity. A provider may see the ordinary technical connection address of any internet request. We do not send them your profile or private choices.

Permissions

  • Internet: to load prices, indicators, and market headlines.
  • Notifications: to show a local alert when a price reaches the level you set. You can deny this permission. Alerts still stay inside the app.
  • Background work: to check your saved alerts on a schedule while the app is closed. This is not used to track you.

News links open in the browser. That site’s own policy then applies.

Children

The app is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect children’s data.
